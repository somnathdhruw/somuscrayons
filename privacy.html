Privacy Policy: Somu’s Crayons

Effective date: November 25, 2026

In short: the app does not collect personal information. Your pages, your artwork and your settings stay on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Somu’s Crayons (“the app”) is provided by Somnath Mandavi, India. You can contact us at somuscrayons.support@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in your browser or app storage, on your own device only:

  • Profile names and whether a profile is for an adult or a child
  • Your region choice and paper size
  • The parent PIN and the daily time limit
  • Time used each day, for the time limit and the parent progress view
  • The pages you add, your artwork, and saved colours

We cannot see this information, and it is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time with “Delete data” in the Parent zone, or by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share information, because we do not collect it.
  • We do not use your location, contacts or microphone.
  • There is no chat and no public sharing of artwork.

4. Photos and the camera

When you choose an image or take a photo to use as a colouring page, it is processed on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app only asks for access to your photos or camera when you choose to add an image.

5. Services we rely on

The app uses your device’s own fonts and loads nothing from other companies, apart from the hosting provider below.

  • Hosting. The app is delivered by a web hosting provider. Like any website, the provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages. We do not receive or use this data.

6. Children

The app can be used by people of all ages. A Kids profile has simpler tools. Printing, sharing, deleting pages and extra time need the parent PIN, and a parent can set a daily time limit.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children do not create accounts. We have designed the app with children’s privacy rules in mind, such as COPPA in the United States and the Children’s Code in the United Kingdom.

7. Your choices

  • You can delete all pages and artwork from the Parent zone.
  • You can save a backup file of your data to your device and restore it later. You control where that file is kept.
  • You can save, print or share your artwork. Sharing uses your device’s own share menu, and what you share is your choice.

8. Changes

If we add features that collect information, such as accounts, cloud backup or purchases, we will update this policy first. We will ask a parent for permission before collecting any information from a child. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email somuscrayons.support@gmail.com.
